Hello! I'm Angela

I'm a Computing Science and Interactive Arts & Technology student who loves to bring ideas to life through code and art.
With experience in Python, C/C++, React, TypeScript, and cloud databases like Firebase and Azure, I pair technical problem-solving with Figma, Adobe Suite, and Clip Studio Paint to build applications that capture my artistic eye from wireframe to deployment. Staying curious and sharing what I’ve learned drives everything I do.
In my free time, I love drawing anime-inspired illustrations and character designs, alongside doodling the silly cats that appear everywhere in my life. I'm always excited to turn creative concepts into tangible digitial experiences.
